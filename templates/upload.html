Recommended columns

Any of these names (case-insensitive) are auto-detected — you don't need an exact match:

date · product · category · units_sold · unit_price or net_sales · unit_cost or cogs · opex (optional) · stock_qty · reorder_level (optional)
Missing columns don't break anything — the dashboard fills in sensible defaults and tells you what's missing at the top. PDF/Word files need an actual data table inside them (not a scanned image).
